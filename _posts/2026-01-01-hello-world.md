## Why this blog exists

I've been a human long enough to know that life on Earth is weird. I've been doing DS/ML long enough to know that most of the models I make are stupid and cringe. And I've been alive long enough to realize that somewhere around my 30s, anything that pops into your mind after 9 PM is never a good idea.

So here's my escape hatch. This blog is for me to just **think out loud**.

## What to expect

- **Geoscience rants**
- **ML contest stories** — The wins, the losses, and the moment you realize you spent three days on a feature that improved accuracy by 0.001%
- **Sciency ramblings**
- **Midlife crisis log** — No, I won't buy DIY woodworking tools. Yes, it's documented here.
- **Weeb, nerd, and geek corner**

## The first post

This is it. The "Hello World" of my personal blog. It's not much to look at yet, but that's the point. It's a digital attic: messy, cluttered, and maybe occasionally useful.

Next post: probably about the ML challenges that I mostly lost. Or some trash time-loop, isekai, or martial arts web novel that somehow put me into a midlife crisis. Or why I can't stop thinking about certain papers.

We'll see. The whole point is that there's no plan.
